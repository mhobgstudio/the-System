## How to Use

1. Play each sample (open in any media player)
2. Note your favorite male and female voices
3. Update the quote generation config with your chosen voice names

Voice names are the filenames without `.mp3`. For Edge-TTS these are the full voice names like `en-US-GuyNeural`.
