RELIGIOUS PROCEDURES (DETAILED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NAFILAH (OPTIONAL SALAT) PROCEDURES:

SUNDAY DAY: Prays 4 rak'ats. Recite Fatiha and Amama Rasul in each.

SUNDAY NIGHT: Prays 20 rak'ats. Fatiha (1x), Ikhlas (50x), Falaq (1x), Nas (1x). 
    * Post-Salat: Astagfirul-Lahal-Azim (100x), Allahumma rab'bigfirli... (100x), Salat ala Nabiyyi (100x), La hawla wala quw-wata... (7x), La manja... (7x), Ash-hadu an la ilaha... (7x).

MONDAY DAY: Prays 2 rak'ats. Fatiha (1x), Kursi (1x), Ikhlas (1x), Falaq (1x), Nas (1x). 
    * Post-Salat: Astagfirul-lahal-Azim (20x), Salat alan Nabiyyi (20x).

MONDAY NIGHT: Prays 4 rak'ats. Fatiha + Ikhlas (1st: 10x, 2nd: 20x, 3rd: 30x, 4th: 40x). 
    * Post-Salat: Ikhlas (75x), Allahumma Rab big firli... (75x).

TUESDAY DAY: Prays 10 rak'ats. Fatiha, Ayatul Kursi, Ikhlas (3x) in each.

TUESDAY NIGHT: Prays 2 rak'ats. Fatiha, Qadr (6x), Ikhlas (7x) in each.

WEDNESDAY DAY: Prays 12 rak'ats. Fatiha, Ayatul Kursi, Ikhlas.

THURSDAY NIGHT: Between Maghrib and Isha, prays 2 rak'ats. Fatiha, Kursi, Ikhlas, Falaq, Nas (5x each). 
    * Post-Salat: Astagfirul-lahal-Azim (15x).

FRIDAY DAY: Prays 4 rak'ats before Imam starts sermon. Fatiha, Ikhlas (50x) in each.

FRIDAY NIGHT: Between Maghrib and Isha, prays 12 rak'ats. Fatiha (1x), Ikhlas (12x), Falaq, Nas.

SATURDAY DAY: Prays 4 rak'ats. Fatiha (1x), Ikhlas (3x). Post: Ayatul Kursi.

SATURDAY NIGHT: Between Maghrib and Isha, prays 12 rak'ats.

SALATU TASBIH: 4 rak'ats, 300 Tasbihs total.
    * Dhikr: "Subhanal-lahi wal hamdu lilahi wala ilaha il'lal lahu wal-lahi Akbar."
    * Procedure per Rak'at: 15x after Surah, 10x in Ruku, 10x after Ruku (standing), 10x in Sujud, 10x in sitting between Sujuds, 10x in 2nd Sujud, 10x in Tashaud/sitting before standing.

ISTIKHARA: Perform 2 rak'ats Nafila. Say Dua after completing or in Sujud of last rak'at. Mention need at "hadha (mention your need)".
